Rules

How To Play

The game challenges you to match 7 colors of the pride flag sequentially: Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Violet. You have 30 seconds for each. Snap a photo of an object in your room or environment that matches the color.

Scoring

To discourage simply placing the lens directly on a flat colored screen or surface, the scoring is based on three parameters:

  • Color Coverage (Max 500 pts): The target color should cover 33% to 85% of the frame. If it fills less than 5% or more than 95% (like an extreme close-up), you receive severe penalties!
  • Detail & Texture (Max 300 pts): Real objects (leaves, flowers, mugs) contain clear lines and textures. Blank walls or screens have zero texture and score poorly.
  • Shade Variations (Max 200 pts): Real objects have shadows and highlights, creating a rich color landscape. Flat objects score 0.
